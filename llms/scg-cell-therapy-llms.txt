# SCG Cell Therapy

> SCG Cell Therapy Pte. Ltd. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company headquartered in
> Singapore, with operations across Singapore, China and Germany, developing novel
> immunotherapies for infectious diseases and the cancers they cause. It is a therapeutics
> developer, not a software vendor.

SCG targets the most common cancer-causing infections — Helicobacter pylori, human
papillomavirus (HPV), hepatitis B virus (HBV) and Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) — and is
advancing a pipeline of TCR-engineered T cell therapies, antibodies and therapeutic
vaccines built on its proprietary GianTCR platform. Lead programs include SCG101, a
first-in-class autologous HBV-specific TCR-T therapy targeting hepatitis B surface antigen
in HBV-related hepatocellular carcinoma, and SCG142, a next-generation HPV E7-specific
TCR-T therapy for HPV-associated solid tumors that received FDA IND clearance.
